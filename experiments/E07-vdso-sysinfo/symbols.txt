 3. auxv contract for i386 processes (ARCH_DLINFO_IA32, arch/x86/include/asm/elf.h:289-296):

    AT_SYSINFO      (32) = vdso_base + vdso32_image.sym___kernel_vsyscall  (== base + e_entry)
    AT_SYSINFO_EHDR (33) = vdso_base
    Both are emitted; AT_SYSINFO is consumed by glibc only when AT_SYSINFO_EHDR != NULL
    (sysdeps/unix/sysv/linux/dl-parse_auxv.h:57-61); fallback GLRO(dl_sysinfo) = e_entry + l_addr
    (elf/setup-vdso.h:109-110); final fallback _dl_sysinfo_int80 = "int $0x80; ret".
